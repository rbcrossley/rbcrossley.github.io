# Problem statement
AI is taking over the world.
AI is definitely reducing the required number of tech personnel everywhere around the world.
AI is impacting Nepal already.
AI has already impacted those people who used to work in off-shored companies within Nepal.
Their jobs have been gone. Or maybe new hiring has been stopped. Or maybe something else. It has definitely impacted the people in the IT industry.

The uncertainty is real. The fear is real. AI is actually reducing the net jobs in the market.
