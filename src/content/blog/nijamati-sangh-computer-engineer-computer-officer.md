# Introduction

Sangh Computer Engineer is an extremely powerful position you can get as a computer engineer. It is a policy building role if I am not wrong. Along with lots of IT functions that you definitely have to perform there. You work in line with various ministries of Government of Nepal.


# Why sangh?

- get to participate in national level IT and cyber security policy building.
- most powerful position you can hold as a computer engineer in nepal government.
- work directly to make impact to the general public along with ministries of government of nepal
- salary is also decent. It is around 58,000 per month.

# Day to day life in Sangh
I do not have exact idea on how the day to day life looks like as a sangh computer engineer.

- First paper: GK,IQ,Technical MCQs---100

Technical portion for computer engineer seems to consist of:

**Section-A:**

- computer networks
- coa and microprocessors
- digital design
- basic electrical and electronics
    - electrical
    - electronics
- principles of electronic communications


**Section-B:**

- structured and object oriented programming
- data structures
- software engineering principles(SAAD)
- dbms
- operating system


**Section-C:**

- AI
- Compiler Design
- CG
- Emerging technology and electives
- Technical Writing and Case study(one is for writing proposal for a specific situation, another is related to case study in software engineering and dbms and os domains.)



The best resources are the technical books of specific subjects. Do not think you can crack loksewa simply by mugging up notes except in exceptional scenarios(for example: of current scenario where there are too many vacancies and possibly very few people).
