# Introduction

NRB is the most lucrative government job for freshers in Nepal. Unlike private jobs where you start at the bottom and have to gain experience to go up the hierarchy, NRB offers a different path. 

NRB is better even when compared with other government banks jobs because NRB does not require a masters degree for officer level positions.

As an IT graduate, you can apply for Assistant Director(IT) in NRB.


# Why Nepal Rastra Bank
Nepal Rastra Bank job is a stable government job. It provides you with job security. Pension does not exist anymore though but there are various retirement benefits. 

It is (almost) future proof. 

It pays really high. Without including bonuses, NRB 6th level officers were getting 1 lakh 5 thousands before the 21% increment.

With 21% increment, the salary moves towards 1,27,050. (Obviously it will not be exactly same because how they increase 21% will depend on the corporation to corporation basis.).

Now, NRB also provides annual bonuses. Usually 3-6 months of salary is given as bonuses. Assume, you get 3 months of salary as bonuses. And note that, NRB is the lender of last resort and it is very stable because its goal itself is to make Nepal's economy stable, so NRB will never be in loss. If it is in loss, Nepal is done (bro!).

The salary after 21% increment will be around 70,000 per month. Extra 3 months of salary means, your total package will be:

1,27,050 x 12+70,000 x 3

i.e., you will make around 17.5 Lakhs per annum. Not bad by any means.

# Vacancy

Will NRB vacancy for IT Assistant Director open this year?

Nobody knows except those who work at NRB themselves(they might). The signals are off. NRB provided the updated syllabus in their website for Assistant Director (Administration), but have not yet updated the syllabus for IT or Cybersecurity.

![NRB Career Notice shows no possible vacancy for IT AD](/images/blog/nrb-career-notice.png)


But there is hope as well. 

IT development and operations is left under the supervision of Governor. And we know that NRB has got a latest governor. So, the chances are there that he might want to open lots of IT vacancies. It could be in the form of Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence or basic IT as well.


You will get good promotion prospects. NRB is absolutely top-most respected job in Nepal.


# Work You'll Actually Do at NRB as Assistant Director(IT)

The work that you will do as a Assistant Director (IT or Cyber Security) in NRB will be mostly related to SupTech, RegTech. Your task is to audit bank's "defense" against cyber threats. Your task it ensure that the cyber resilience guidelines, IT guidelines, AI guidelines, etc., are being followed properly in A class banks.

## IT 

### 1st paper
- coa and microprocessors
- digital logic 
- operating systems
- dbms
- computer networks
- distributed systems
- cryptography and network security

There will be 25 marks objective and 75 marks descriptive exam. This pattern will continue for all three papers.

### 2nd paper
- programming language
- dsa
- discrete mathematics
- compiler design
- SAAD
- software engineering
- MIS and web engineering
- computer graphics


### 3rd paper
- IT in central bank(NRB) and Nepalese banking sector (history and benefits type)
- Relevant Acts, Policies, and Guidelines(ICT policy, NRB Act, ETA, IT Guidelines etc)
- Internet and network programmming
- cyber security and ethics
- overview of fintech, regtech, and suptech
- e-governance
- aiml
- cloud computing
- development in virtual currency
