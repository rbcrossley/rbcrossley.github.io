# Eligibility
You must complete Bachelors Degree in Computer Engineering to be eligible for this post.

You must be betwen 21-35 years(if you're a male). Females get opportunity till 40.

# Form fee

The form fee for Computer Engineering Level 7 at NEA is NPR 1,200.

For inclusion quota, you pay extra Rs.400 for the Level 7.

# Probation Period
Like any other goverenment corporations, the probation period at Nepal Electricity Authority is 1 year for male, 6 months for female.

Probation period is like a trial period at NEA after it hires you.
